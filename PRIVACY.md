# RuEn Translator privacy policy

*Last updated: 14 November 2026*

RuEn Translator translates documents from Russian to English entirely on your computer.
**It does not collect, store, send or share any personal information, and it does not use the
internet.**

## Your documents

The documents you open are read from your disk, translated on your computer and saved where you
choose. They are never uploaded anywhere. While a translation is in progress, a working copy is
kept in your Windows temporary folder; it is deleted when you close the app.

## What stays on your computer

RuEn Translator saves two small files so it can work better next time:

- your glossary of terms (`glossary.txt`), if you add any
- how fast your computer translated earlier documents (`speed.json`), used for the time-left
  estimate

They are kept in `%APPDATA%\RuEnTranslator` (for the Microsoft Store version, in the app's private
storage). They never leave your computer. To delete them, uninstall the app or delete that folder.

## No network access

RuEn Translator has no accounts, analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking, and it makes no
network connections. The translation and text-recognition models are installed with the app.
The only links are in **About** (the source code page on github.com and the licence), and they
open in your web browser only when you click them. GitHub's privacy statement applies there.

## Children

RuEn Translator collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this ever changes, this policy will be updated before the change is released.
