## Product name
RuEn Translator

## Short description *(shown at the top of the listing)*
Translate Russian documents and scans to English on your PC: Word, PDF, text and scanned pages, fully offline and private.

## Description
RuEn Translator turns Russian documents into English right on your computer. Nothing is uploaded: the translation engine and the text-recognition models are built in, so it works without an internet connection, and your documents never leave your PC.

Drop a document on the window, look through it, click Translate, and download the English version.

DOCUMENTS IN, DOCUMENTS OUT
• Word (.docx): headings, bold text, tables, lists, headers and footers keep their formatting
• PDF: the English text replaces the Russian on the same page, so the layout stays as it was, or get an editable Word document instead
• Text files, including old Windows-1251 and KOI8-R files

SCANNED PAGES TOO
• Scanned PDFs and images (PNG, JPG, TIFF, BMP) are read with built-in Russian text recognition (OCR)
• Tilted scans are straightened automatically
• Justified text, tables and multi-column rows are kept together, so sentences are translated in context

SEE WHAT YOU'RE TRANSLATING
• Preview the Russian document before you start, and the English result before you save it
• A progress bar shows what's happening and the time left, and learns your PC's speed
• Cancel at any time

YOUR TERMS, TRANSLATED YOUR WAY
• Add your own glossary (Russian term = English term) for technical or company vocabulary
• Built-in handling of legal and government terms: kray, oblast, autonomous okrug, Council of the Federation, Prosecutor General
• Quick text: paste a few sentences and translate them instantly

PRIVATE AND OPEN
RuEn Translator has no accounts, no tracking, no ads and no network connection. It's free and open source under the GNU AGPL v3, and the source code is on GitHub.

Translation quality: on the Russian Constitution (a 76-page scan), the translation scores 71 chrF / 44 BLEU against the official English text. It's built for understanding documents quickly. For legal or official use, have important translations checked by a professional translator.

## Product features *(up to 20, each up to 200 characters)*
1. Translates Russian to English fully offline: no uploads, no account, no internet needed
2. Word documents keep their formatting: headings, bold, tables, lists, headers and footers
3. PDFs keep their page layout, or export to an editable Word document
4. Built-in OCR reads scanned PDFs and photos of pages (PNG, JPG, TIFF, BMP)
5. Straightens tilted scans automatically
6. Preview the Russian document and the English result before saving
7. Progress bar with an estimated time left
8. Your own glossary for technical and company terms
9. Built-in legal and government terminology
10. Quick text translation for a few sentences
11. Reads old Russian text encodings (Windows-1251, KOI8-R, CP866)
12. Batch mode from the command line: ruentranslator --translate a.pdf b.docx --out C:\folder
13. Free and open source (GNU AGPL v3)
